Pricing

Is Bankroll Pro Poker free?

It's free to download and try. You can log real sessions and see your stats before paying anything. When you're ready, a single in-app purchase unlocks the full app.

Is there a subscription?

No. The unlock is a one-time purchase. There are no monthly or yearly fees, and nothing renews.

What happens to my sessions when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Everything you logged during the trial is still there after you unlock the full app.

I got a new phone. Do I have to pay again?

No. Sign in to the new phone with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy the unlock, then tap Restore Purchase in the app.

Are there ads?

Never. Not in the free trial and not in the full version.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Your sessions are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no login and no email required.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Bankroll Pro Poker works fully offline, so you can log a session in a card room with no signal.

If I delete the app, can you recover my data?

No. Because your data lives only on your device, we never have a copy. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Using the app

Does it track cash games and tournaments?

Both. Cash sessions track profit and win rate. Tournaments track your total cost, return on investment and how often you cash.

What is bb/hr?

Big blinds per hour — the standard way to measure a cash game win rate across different stakes. It's your profit divided by the big blind, divided by hours played. Winning $100 in 4 hours at a $2 big blind is 12.5 bb/hr.

How are tournament ROI and ITM% calculated?

Your total cost for each tournament includes the buy-in, fee, rebuys and add-on. ROI is your total cashes minus your total cost, divided by total cost, across all your tournaments. ITM% is the share of tournaments where you finished in the money.

Which phones does it work on?

Android now, on Google Play. iPhone is coming soon to the App Store.

Is this a gambling app?

No. Bankroll Pro Poker is a record-keeping tool. It doesn't offer real-money games or wagering of any kind — it just helps you track results from games you play elsewhere.

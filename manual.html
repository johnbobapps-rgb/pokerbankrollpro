    Bankroll Pro Poker

    User Manual VERSION 1.0.1

    Bankroll Pro Poker is a private results tracker for poker players. Log cash games and tournaments, plan trips, track travel expenses with receipt photos, and export your data. Everything is stored on your device: no account, no cloud sync, no ads.

    PLATFORMiOS & Android
    LANGUAGES10 supported
    CURRENCIES32 worldwide

    1Getting Started

    First Launch

    Bankroll Pro Poker opens ready to use. It comes pre‑loaded with more than 30 poker locations and their card rooms, from Las Vegas and Los Angeles to Atlantic City, Florida and Texas, plus entries for WSOP Europe, Online Poker and Home Game. No account or internet connection is required.

    On first launch you're asked to choose your language from the 10 the app supports. Tap one and the whole app switches.

    TIPYou can change your language any time later in Settings → Language.

    Free to Try

    The app is free to download and try. You can log up to 10 sessions or 3 trips, whichever comes first. After that, adding new sessions or trips needs the full version, which is a single one‑time purchase with no subscription and no ads.

    • Until you unlock, the home screen shows how much of the trial is left, with an Unlock button beside it.
    • Everything you've already logged stays yours. Viewing, editing and exporting your data always stay free.
    • To unlock, tap Unlock on the home screen or Settings → Unlock Full Version.
    • If you've bought the full version before, for example on a new phone, tap Settings → Restore Purchase. Once unlocked, Settings shows Full Version ✓ Unlocked.

    Glossary

    TermDefinition
    SessionOne sitting at the tables: a cash game, a tournament, or anything else you play.
    FormatThe kind of game. The app ships with Cash Game and Tournament; you can add your own.
    VariantThe specific game within a format, such as NLHE, PLO or NLHE Freezeout.
    Money InWhat you put on the table: your cash buy‑ins, or your total tournament cost.
    Money OutWhat you left with: your cash‑out, or your tournament payout.
    NetMoney out minus money in.
    TripA planned poker trip with a destination, dates and a budget.
    LocationA city or region where you play (e.g. Las Vegas, NV).
    VenueA specific card room within a location (e.g. Aria, Bellagio).
    ExpenseA non‑poker cost of a trip, such as hotel, flights or food, logged against that trip.
    True ResultA trip's poker net minus its expenses: what the trip really put in or took out of your pocket.
    ROIReturn on investment: your net as a percentage of what you paid in.
    ITM"In the money": a tournament where you cashed.

    2Home Screen

    The home screen is your main hub. It shows your overall results and gives you quick access to every feature.

    Stats Bar

    At the top you'll see your overall performance at a glance:

    • Total net — your total win or loss across all sessions, in large green (win) or red (loss) text.
    • Sessions — the number of sessions logged.
    • Total Bet — the sum of everything you've put in.
    • Avg $/hr — your average win or loss per hour across all timed sessions.
    • Trips — the number of trips created.
    • Best Win — your single best session. Swipe the stats row sideways to see it.

    These stats reflect your poker results only. Trip expenses are tracked separately and never change your total net.

    Main Menu

    The menu is grouped into sessions, trips and expenses, with settings at the bottom:

    ButtonWhat it does
    Add SessionLog a cash game or tournament.
    View SessionsBrowse, filter and edit all your sessions.
    Add TripPlan a poker trip with a destination, dates and budget.
    View TripsSee your trips with the sessions and expenses inside them.
    Add ExpenseLog a trip cost such as hotel, flights or food.
    View ExpensesSee all your expenses, grouped by trip.
    SettingsFormats, venues, preferences, security and exports.
    TIPDon't track travel costs? Turn off Settings → Show Expenses and the expenses group disappears from the home screen. Turn it back on any time; nothing is deleted.

    Getting Around

    Every screen has a back link at the top left, such as ‹ Menu or ‹ Settings. Your data is saved automatically as you go. When you leave the app from the home screen, it asks "Exit Bankroll Pro Poker?" first.

    3Adding a Session

    Tap Add Session on the home screen to log a cash game or tournament. If you try to save with a required field empty, the field is highlighted and the app asks you to fill it in.

    Quick Add

    Location, venue, format and variant are dropdowns. If what you need isn't listed, tap + Quick Add beside the dropdown, type the name, and tap +. It's added to your lists everywhere in the app.

    Session Time Tracking

    Tap ▶ Start when you sit down to stamp the current time, and ■ Stop when you finish, or type the times in yourself. With both times entered, the app shows how long you played and your win or loss per hour. Timed sessions count toward your Avg $/hr. Times are optional.

    Pending Sessions

    Still playing, or waiting on a result? Leave Money Out blank and save. The session is marked as pending until you edit it and enter the amount.

    Tournament details

    Tap Tournament details to open a small calculator for tournament costs:

    FieldWhat to enter
    Buy-inThe part of your entry that goes to the prize pool.
    Fee / rakeThe house fee on top of the buy‑in.
    Rebuy $ · # RebuysThe cost of one rebuy and how many you took.
    Add-onThe cost of any add‑on.
    Field sizeHow many entries the tournament had.
    Finish placeWhere you finished.

    As you type, the app adds up your total cost — buy‑in, fee, rebuys and add‑on — and fills it into Money In for you. Enter your payout in Money Out and it shows your ROI for the tournament and whether you finished in the money (ITM ✓) or not (No cash).

    Active Trip and Last Used

    If today falls inside one of your trips, the app sets the location to the trip's destination and tells you which trip is active. Otherwise it remembers the location, venue, format and variant you used last time, so regular games take only a few taps to log.

    4Viewing Sessions

    Tap View Sessions to see every session you've logged.

    Summary Bar

    The top of the screen shows your total net, number of sessions, total bet and average hourly rate. Once you've logged completed tournaments, a Tournaments line appears beneath it with the number played, your overall tournament ROI, and your ITM percentage — the share of tournaments in which you cashed.

    Session Cards

    Each session card shows:

    • An icon for the format, and the venue name.
    • Location, format, variant, and your stakes or buy‑in label.
    • Money in, money out, and the date.
    • The net result in green (win) or red (loss). Pending sessions show ⏳ instead.
    • For tournaments with details entered, your finish and ROI.
    • A currency badge, such as EUR, if the session was played in a currency other than your home currency.

    Filtering Sessions

    Two filters sit at the top. The format filter shows only one format, or only sessions that are still pending. The date filter offers Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Last 90 Days, This Year, or Custom Range…. They can be combined, and a totals bar shows the net and hourly rate for just the sessions on screen. Tap ✕ Clear to remove the date filter.

    Editing a Session

    Tap any session card to open it for editing. Change any field and tap Save Changes, or tap Delete Session to remove it.

    WARNINGDeleted sessions cannot be recovered.

    5Adding a Trip

    Tap Add Trip to plan a poker trip. A trip groups your sessions and expenses for one visit and tracks them against a budget.

    FieldDescription
    Trip NameA name for the trip (e.g. Vegas Weekend).
    DestinationWhere you're going. This is the same list as your locations; use + Quick Add to add a new one.
    Date RangeThe first and last day of the trip.
    Money InYour total budget for the trip.

    How Sessions Link to Trips

    You don't assign sessions to a trip yourself. A session belongs to a trip automatically when its date falls within the trip's dates and its location matches the trip's destination.

    Expenses and Trips

    Expenses work the other way round: you choose the trip when you add the expense. See Trip Expenses.

    Duplicate Trip Protection

    The app won't let two trips use the same destination on overlapping dates. If you try, a warning names the trip that already covers that period and asks you to choose different dates or a different destination.

    6Viewing Trips

    Tap View Trips to see all your trips with their sessions and expenses.

    Trip Cards

    Each trip card shows the trip name and destination, its dates and number of nights, the budget, and how many sessions it holds.

    Expanding a Trip

    Trips are collapsed to keep the list short. Tap the trip's budget amount to expand or collapse it, or use Show All / Hide All at the top right to open or close every trip at once. An expanded trip shows:

    • Its sessions — venue, format, variant, date, times and result. Tap one to edit it.
    • A + Expense button and the expenses logged against the trip. Tap an expense to edit it.
    • The breakdown — Poker Net, Expenses and True Result.

    Sessions that don't fall inside any trip are listed together under Unassigned Sessions.

    Budget Alerts

    With Settings → Budget Alerts on, the app warns you on the home screen when your losses on the current trip reach a set percentage of its budget. Choose the percentage in Settings → Alert Threshold.

    Editing a Trip

    Open a trip to change its name, destination, dates or budget, or tap Delete Trip to remove it.

    7Trip Expenses

    A winning trip can still cost you money once the hotel, flights and meals are counted. Trip expenses let you log those costs against each trip, keep the receipts, and see what the trip really came to.

    Your poker results stay exactly as they were. Expenses are stored separately and only combine with them in a trip's True Result.

    Adding an Expense

    There are three ways to start:

    • From the home screen, tap Add Expense.
    • On the Trip Expenses screen, tap the Add Expense button at the top.
    • In View Trips, expand a trip and tap + Expense.

    You need at least one trip before you can log an expense. Fill in the fields and tap Save Expense.

    The Expenses Screen

    Tap View Expenses on the home screen to see everything you've logged:

    • Summary — your total expenses, with the number of expenses and the number of trips they belong to.
    • Trip headings — expenses are grouped by trip. Each heading shows the trip name, destination, and that trip's expense total.
    • Expense rows — category, date and amount. A 📝 shows the expense has a note.

    Tap any row to edit that expense.

    Expense Fields

    FieldDescriptionRequired

    CategoryThe type of cost.YES
    CurrencyThe currency you paid in. If it isn't your home currency, you'll be asked for the exchange rate.NO
    AmountWhat you paid.YES
    DateThe date of the expense.YES
    Note (optional)Who you paid, what it was for, a confirmation number.NO

    Categories

    The app ships with six categories: Flights, Hotel, Food, Transport, Entertainment and Other. To add your own, tap + Quick Add next to the category dropdown, type a name, and tap +. You can remove categories you don't use from the same panel.

    Receipt Photos

    Under Receipts on the add and edit expense screens there are two buttons:

    IconButtonWhat it does
    📷CameraOpens the camera so you can photograph a receipt on the spot.
    🖼️GalleryPicks one or more existing photos or screenshots from your device, which is useful for emailed confirmations.

    The first time you use them, your phone asks permission to use the camera or photos. You can attach several photos to one expense; each appears as a thumbnail beside the buttons.

    • View — tap a thumbnail to open the photo full screen.
    • Zoom — pinch to zoom in on small print, and drag to move around while zoomed.
    • Remove — tap the ✕ on a thumbnail. The app asks "Remove this receipt photo?" first, so a stray tap won't lose a receipt.
    PRIVACYReceipt photos are stored on your device only, like everything else in the app. They leave your phone only when you export them yourself.

    Editing or Deleting an Expense

    When editing an expense you can change any field and add or remove receipt photos, then tap Save Changes. You can also delete the expense from there.

    WARNINGDeleted expenses and their receipt photos cannot be recovered.

    8Currencies & Exchange Rates

    Bankroll Pro Poker keeps all your results in one home currency, while letting you log individual sessions and expenses in whatever currency you actually played or paid in. That makes a mixed trip — euros at a European stop, dollars at home — add up correctly.

    Your Home Currency

    Tap Settings → Currency to choose from 32 currencies. Its symbol is used for every total in the app and in your exports.

    Playing or Paying in Another Currency

    When adding a session or an expense, the Currency dropdown starts on the currency you used last. When you pick one that isn't your home currency:

    • A rate field appears, labelled like 1 EUR = ? USD. Enter what one unit is worth in your home currency.
    • Type your amounts in the foreign currency, exactly as they were at the table or on the receipt.
    • When you save, the app converts them to your home currency at that rate and stores the result. It also keeps the original amounts for your records.

    The rate you enter is remembered for next time, so you only type it once per trip unless it changes.

    Exchange Rates

    Tap Settings → Exchange Rates to see and edit your saved rates in one place. Use Add a currency… to set a rate before you travel, then tap Save Rates.

    GOOD TO KNOWThe app works offline, so it does not look up live exchange rates. You enter the rate you actually got. Each session and expense is converted when you save it, so updating a rate later affects new entries, not ones already logged.

    9Settings

    Formats, Variants, Venues & Locations

    Formats & Variants. The app ships with two formats. Cash Game includes NLHE, PLO, PLO5, Limit Hold'em, Omaha Hi‑Lo, Seven Card Stud, Razz, Short Deck and mixed games. Tournament includes NLHE Freezeout, NLHE Rebuy, PKO / Bounty, Satellite, Sit & Go, PLO and Mixed. Add your own formats, and your own variants inside each one.

    Venues. Each location has its own list of card rooms. Pick a location, then add or remove venues.

    Locations. The cities and regions used when adding a session and as trip destinations.

    Anything already used by a session or trip can't be removed until those records are changed or deleted, so you can't orphan your history by accident.

    Turn on Settings → App PIN Lock, then enter and confirm a 4‑digit PIN. From then on the app asks for it before showing your results. With the PIN set you can also turn on Use Fingerprint / Face; if biometrics fail, the app falls back to your PIN. Turn App PIN Lock off to remove the PIN.

    10Exporting Your Data

    There are three ways to get your data out of the app. All three are under Data in settings, and nothing leaves your device until you choose where to send it.

    ExportWhat you getBest for
    Export to CSVOne spreadsheet file with your sessions, trips and expenses.Working with the numbers in Excel or Google Sheets.
    Export ZIP (data + photos)The same CSV plus every receipt photo, in one ZIP file.A complete backup, receipts included.
    Export PDF reportA formatted report anyone can open and print.Your records, or handing to someone else.

    Export to CSV

    Tap Settings → Export to CSV and choose where the file should go. The choices depend on your phone:

    OptionDescription
    Share / Save FileOpens the share sheet for iCloud, AirDrop, Google Drive, Dropbox, email and more. (iPhone)
    Save to DownloadsSaves the file to your device's Downloads folder. (Android)
    Send via EmailOpens your email app with the CSV attached. (Android)
    Google Drive / DropboxSends the file to that app. (Android)
    Copy to ClipboardCopies the data so you can paste it straight into Google Sheets, Excel or any app.

    CSV File Format

    The file is divided into three sections, each with a total row. Column headers are exported in your app language:

    SESSIONS
Date, Venue, Location, Format, Variant, Stakes / Buy-in, Start Time, End Time, Duration (mins), Duration (hrs), Money In, Money Out, Net, Avg $/hr, ROI %, Finish place, Note, Currency (logged), Money In (orig), Money Out (orig)

TRIPS
Name, Destination, Start, End, Nights, Budget

EXPENSES
Date, Trip, Category, Net, Currency (logged), Amount (orig), Note

    Amounts are in your home currency; the last columns keep the currency and amounts you originally entered. Your own text, such as venue names and notes, exports exactly as you typed it. The filename includes the export date, for example bankroll_pro_poker_2026-11-06.csv

    Export ZIP (data + photos)

    A CSV is plain text, so it can't hold pictures. To take your receipts with you, tap Export ZIP (data + photos). The app builds one ZIP file and opens your phone's share sheet. Inside you'll find the CSV described above and a receipts folder holding every receipt photo.

    The ZIP is built entirely on your phone, with no internet connection needed. With many photos it can take a moment.

    Export PDF report

    Tap Export PDF report for a formatted report with your overall results followed by tables of your sessions, trips and expenses, each with totals. Like the ZIP, it is built on your phone and handed to the share sheet. The report lists your expenses but doesn't include the receipt photos — use the ZIP export for those.

    11Tips & Tricks

    1Start the clock when you sit down

    Tap ▶ Start as you take your seat and ■ Stop when you rack up. Your hourly rate is only as good as your timed sessions.

    2Save now, finish later

    Log the session the moment you buy in and leave Money Out blank. It waits as pending, and the pending filter finds it again.

    3Let the calculator do tournament maths

    Enter buy‑in, fee, rebuys and add‑on in Tournament details and Money In fills itself. No more forgetting the second bullet.

    4Label your stakes

    Put 1/2, 2/5 or $100+$20 in Stakes / Buy-in. It shows on every session card and in your exports, so you can compare levels later.

    5Create the trip before you travel

    Set up the trip with its destination and budget first. Sessions you log there on those dates group to it automatically.

    6Snap receipts as you go

    Add each expense when you pay and photograph the receipt on the spot. It takes seconds, and you won't be sorting paper after the trip.

    7Set rates before a foreign trip

    Add the currency in Settings → Exchange Rates before you leave, and the rate is already filled in when you log your first session abroad.

    8Back up with the ZIP export

    Everything lives on your phone only. Export a ZIP now and then to Drive, iCloud or email, and your data and receipts are safe if the phone isn't.

About

Made by a player, for players.

Bankroll Pro Poker is built by JohnBobApps, an independent studio run by one developer. The goal is simple: honest tools that do one job well, cost one fair price, and respect your privacy.

Most bankroll apps want a monthly fee, an account, or your data. Bankroll Pro Poker asks for none of those. Your sessions live on your phone, the math is done on your phone, and nobody else sees your results unless you show them.

Part of the JohnBobApps family

Bankroll Pro Poker is the companion to Casino Bankroll Pro, the session and bankroll tracker for table games and slots. Same privacy-first approach, now built around the way poker is actually played — cash sessions measured in big blinds per hour, and tournaments measured by ROI and how often you cash.

Our promises

  • No ads
  • No account required
  • Your data stays on your device

Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JohnBobApps LLC ("we," "us") handles information in the Poker Bankroll Pro mobile app (the "App") and on pokerbankrollpro.app (the "Website").

The short version

The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

Information you enter in the App

Session details you record — such as buy-ins, cash-outs, tournament costs, game types, venues, dates and notes — are saved in local storage on your device. We do not have access to this information, and it is never sent to our servers or any third party.

No accounts, ads or tracking

  • The App does not require an account or login.
  • The App contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • The App does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

The App is sold as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it completely. Deleting the App, or clearing its data in your device settings, permanently removes everything it has stored. We cannot recover deleted data.

The Website

The Website does not use cookies and does not ask for personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Children

The App is intended for adults of legal gambling age in their jurisdiction. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@johnbobapps.com.

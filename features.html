Features

Every number, done right.

Bankroll Pro Poker handles the math so your results reflect what actually happened at the table.

Cash games

Log a session with your buy-in, cash-out, stakes and time played. The app works out the rest.

  • Profit or loss per session, automatically
  • Win rate in big blinds per hour
  • Running bankroll across all sessions
profit = cash-out − buy-in
bb/hr = (profit ÷ big blind) ÷ hours

Tournaments

Tournament costs add up fast. Every entry counts toward your real investment.

  • Total cost = buy-in + fee + rebuys + add-on
  • ROI% across all your tournaments
  • ITM% — how often you cash
ROI% = (total cashes − total cost) ÷ total cost
